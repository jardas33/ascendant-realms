# v0.374 Route Decision

## One primary route

ROUTE B — CUSTOM BARROSAN ENVIRONMENT / TEMPORARY IMPORTED UNITS

## Why this is the safest route

The repository already contains two valuable forms of leverage:

- Barrosan House02 and the human-approved Barn gold asset establish a credible
  granite/slate/timber visual anchor.
- v0.233-v0.239 preserve authored Blender contracts, roof grammar and roster
  planning, while the Quaternius men can keep gameplay prototyping moving.

The v0.373 audit proves that the missing quality is not solved by more object
placement. Route B focuses new art effort on the true blocker: continuous
terrain, river/banks/roads, a complete Barrosan building grammar, resources and
hostile territory. Temporary imported characters avoid making environment work
wait for a full unit commission.

## Expected result and cost

- Visual improvement: high for terrain, settlement, bridge and hostile identity;
  medium for units until the later faction-unit pass.
- Effort: medium-high.
- Cost category: medium initially; high only if a full custom unit family is
  commissioned later.
- Reuse: Barn gold, House02, authored Blender source conventions, existing
  gameplay/fallback, and Quaternius temporary units/props/nature.
- Replace/author: terrain/elevation, river/banks/roads, bridge, roster,
  resources, agricultural dressing and hostile camp.
- Largest risk: making isolated gold buildings without a connected environment
  and overview-scale approval.
