# v0.374 Current Asset Decisions

## KEEP

### Barrosan Barn gold

desktop-spikes/godot-salto/scenes/gold/barrosan/BarrosanBarnGold.tscn and the
v0.355 manifest record a human-approved, hash-registered passive gold asset. It

The v0.330-v0.338 GLB, Blender and granite/slate/timber texture lineage is a

## KEEP WITH BARROSAN REWORK

### v0.233 modular environment kit

The actual GLB and contract contain useful authored modules for terrain, road,
river banks, bridge and landmarks. Its recorded status is partial. Reuse the
module and contract knowledge only after a natural terrain and material pass.

### v0.235 architecture kit

The actual GLB establishes corrected ridge-to-eave roof geometry for keep,
barracks/workshop and mine. Extend its grammar to the missing roster and connect
it to authored terrain before production use.

### v0.236-v0.239 authored source lineage

The Blender sources and art bible contain useful material, vegetation, roster
and role-silhouette decisions. Consolidate, export, import and review as one
connected family before treating them as runtime production assets.

## TEMPORARY PROTOTYPE

### Quaternius intake and v0.370 subset

The Medieval Village, Fantasy Props, Stylized Nature and Ultimate Modular Men
packs are real local sources with CC0 license files. They are appropriate for
blocking, import smoke and temporary units. They are not a coherent Barrosan
family. The v0.370 subset is explicitly a deliberately small non-production
proof subset.

### v0.368 and v0.373 runtime experiments

These scenes remain useful as gameplay/fallback or diagnostic references. v0.373
is specifically a fail-closed visual audit: six iterations scored 34/60. Its
river ribbons, asset pile, small wagon-yard camp and weak resource identity must
not be used as the next production scene foundation.

### Comparator fallback materials

Keep for technical regression and fallback contracts only. They are not final
world art.

## REJECT FOR FINAL ART

No existing source is deleted or moved by this checkpoint. For final-art
purposes, reject the v0.373 composition strategy itself: primitive ribbons,
flat terrain plates, unrelated props and repeated rearrangement. Also reject any
future purchase that meets the rejection criteria in the acquisition brief,
even if isolated screenshots look attractive.

## UNKNOWN

None for the reviewed local groups. The source files, formats and license
evidence were sufficient for this planning decision. Future unreviewed packs
must remain UNKNOWN until their actual files and terms are inspected.
