## Result

READY FOR HUMAN V0380 CORRECTED HIGHLAND INFRASTRUCTURE REVIEW

This result is ready for human visual judgment only. It is not production acceptance and does not authorize v0.381 or any production migration.

## Iteration decision

Three deterministic presentation iterations were rendered from the same exact GLB. Iteration 1 and iteration 2 were rejected because the primary framing exposed map-edge wedges. Iteration 3 changed only bounded camera framing and restrained value/lighting settings; it removed the visible primary-frame boundary while preserving the corrected source geometry.

## Visible checks in iteration 3

- terrain relief reads broad and smooth rather than corrugated;
- the river is continuous across the frame and visibly continues beneath the bridge;
- the road approaches both bridge landings without occupying the river channel;
- the bridge deck, planks, rails, beams, supports, and granite landings read as one crossing;
- the primary frame has no visible map boundary;
- the six captures are real rendered 1920x1080 PNGs with no HUD, title card, debug overlay, or black frame.

## Limitations for human review

The supplied source remains a small infrastructure-only slice with sparse dressing and simplified authored materials. It does not prove buildings, units, HUD integration, gameplay, or production-wide Salto conversion. Those remain out of scope for v0.380.
