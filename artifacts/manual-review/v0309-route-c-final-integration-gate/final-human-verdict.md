# Final human verdict

The terrain-contact pass improves transitions, water comparisons are readable, and the worker/militia families are more authored. However, at the principal gameplay frame the terrain still reads as a stylized constructed island, water remains a colored channel rather than a convincing river, and the 12-unit formation retains a toy-like repeated low-poly character. The visual gap to v0.141 and the production-readiness gap remain too large for integration.

**Final decision: PIVOT ROUTE C.**
