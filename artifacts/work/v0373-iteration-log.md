# v0.373 Autonomous Visual Iteration Log

## Iteration 1

Rendered frames: `artifacts/work/v0373-iteration-01/`

### Honest critique

This is denser and more intentional than v0.372: the settlement, crossing, road
network, and hostile side occupy one frame. However, it is still not a human
review candidate. The settlement roofs overlap into one oversized orange mass,
the resource deposit is not immediately legible in the primary view, the camp
still reads as props and fence pieces rather than a defensive enclosure, and
the river/bank/approach layers create long parallel strips. Broad zone polygons
have visibly hard edges and read as test patches. Two trees clip at the top of
the primary frame. The camera is closer, but the composition still has too much
empty olive foreground and insufficient believable terrain transition.

### Most damaging remaining defects

1. Overlapping roof silhouettes collapse the settlement hierarchy.
2. Resource area is not readable without the closer frame.
3. Camp perimeter lacks a strong enclosed defensive silhouette.
4. River, banks, and bridge approaches still read as parallel ribbons.
5. Zone polygons have hard angular boundaries.
6. Tree crowns/trunks crop at the top edge.

## Iteration 5

Rendered frames: `artifacts/work/v0373-iteration-05/`

### Honest critique

The river now leaves the frame without a visible cap, the ore mass is smaller,
and the top-edge tree problem is reduced. This pass exposed a material-pipeline
failure: applying a flat material override to the Quaternius roof assets strips
their authored tile texture and produces blank orange roof planes. That change
is rejected and reverted. Even with the override removed, the underlying
composition remains below the human-quality threshold: the settlement still
looks like a tightly packed asset cluster, the river is still a geometric band,
and the camp still depends on a wagon and fence rectangle for identity.

## Iteration 6 and final gate decision

a small fenced wagon yard; the resource deposit remains visually awkward beside
the settlement; and the composition leaves too much unmeaningful foreground.

created. The rejected intermediate frames remain private under `artifacts/work`
