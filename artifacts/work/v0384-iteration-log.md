# v0.384 first inhabited crossing iteration log

## Iteration 2 — yard/style normalization

Rendered real 1920x1080 captures under `desktop-spikes/godot-salto/artifacts/work/v0384-iteration-02/`.

Corrections:

1. Pulled the whole settlement cluster back from the bridge.
2. Opened the west landing and retained an asymmetrical, quieter opposite bank.

Remaining defects:

1. The footpath still read as a hard rectangular strip.
2. The primary shadow concealed too much yard surface.

Disposition: rejected and retained for audit; no final pack inclusion.

## Iteration 3 — final hierarchy and presentation

Rendered real 1920x1080 captures under `desktop-spikes/godot-salto/artifacts/work/v0384-iteration-03/` and promoted to the final runtime/review pack.

Corrections:

1. Replaced the boxy path with an irregular tapered mesh connection.
2. Added restrained non-shadowing yard fill light while preserving directional key-light hierarchy.
3. Kept the bridge, both landings, river, primary, subordinate, yard, and three figures in one stable oblique RTS frame.

Final visual assessment: ready for human review as a bounded first inhabited target, with the explicit limitation that the buildings and humans are authored low-poly feasibility geometry rather than production gold assets.
