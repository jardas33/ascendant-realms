## Six independently observable design cues

1. **Two-floor functional split:** lower agricultural/storage or livestock floor, upper domestic floor (ICNF; corroborated by the porch reference).
2. **Granite-dominant wall plane:** irregular but calm local stone rhythm with selective larger corner/opening stones (primary anchor and masonry reference).
3. **Slate roof and timber eaves/doors:** dark, weathered roof plane and restrained timber accents (primary anchor, porch reference, ICNF).
4. **Exterior stair to an actual upper landing:** the porch reference visibly connects a stone stair to a timber upper access zone; House 02 turns this into a real stair, landing, and door.
5. **Domestic opening rhythm:** small, deeply framed upper openings with stone lintels and sills; no defensive slit-window or tower language (primary anchor and masonry reference).
6. **Modest vernacular asymmetry:** functional additions and porch/stair relationships create variation without fortress massing (all sources, with the ICNF account describing variation by function and local mason).

## Design statement

House 02 is based primarily on 30989 Montesinho A beautiful stone house (54963502273), using 30991 Montesinho masonry, 30987 Rustic porch in Montesinho, and the ICNF Parque Natural de Montesinho built-heritage account only for masonry rhythm, stair/porch construction, slate/timber material cues, and the two-floor agricultural/domestic functional split.

The three downloaded photographs are CC-licensed documentary references. The ICNF page is used as research text only. The resulting Blender asset is separately authored from primitives and authored materials; the v0.329 House 01 source, mesh, GLB, and runtime scene are not inputs.
