# Content and asset pipeline

Content belongs in data and must pass content validation. Visual assets belong in manifests with stable IDs, source/license status, scale metadata, intended use, and review state. Reference-only material is never silently promoted to runtime.

Preferred sequence:

1. author or intake a small candidate;
2. record provenance and approval status;
3. validate IDs, references, source, and runtime slots;
4. integrate behind an opt-in or isolated path;
5. capture at gameplay scale;
6. obtain independent visual review;
7. promote only after the evidence gate passes.
