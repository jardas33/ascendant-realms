# v0.374 Minimum Viable Barrosan Art Kit

This is the minimum coherent kit required before another serious visual scene
is attempted. Counts are production minimums, not a final game-wide roster.

## Nature

Minimum: 2 deciduous tree families with 3 sizes each; 1 conifer family with 3
sizes; 3 bush variants; 3 grass variants; 2 reed variants; 6 small rocks; 4
medium rocks; 2 large formations; 2 restrained flower/colour accents; 2 dead or
hostile vegetation variants. Quaternius satisfies temporary coverage, not final
cohesion. The missing piece is a unified temperate Barrosan treatment and
composed placement grammar.

## Props and resource sites

Minimum: 2 carts, 3 barrels, 3 crates, 2 sack groups, 2 tool groups, 4 fence
modules, 2 gates, 3 barricades, 2 tents/shelters, 1 campfire, 3 resource-working
props, 3 agricultural props, 1 bridge dressing set and 6 settlement clutter
groups. Each resource needs one unmistakable outcrop/body, extraction prop and
drop-off relationship. Current props cover temporary dressing but not authored
resource identity or hostile purpose.

## Units and animation

Minimum: 1 Worker, 1 Militia, 1 Ashen Raider, 1 commander-capable rig and 1
ranged/specialist rig. Each needs idle, walk, work/brace or aim, attack/action,
hit and death where gameplay requires it, plus selection-safe feet and faction
recognition. The Quaternius men supply temporary rigged characters; final
Barrosan and Ashen silhouettes remain a gap.

## Gate before the next scene

Do not start another visual scene until the first connected slice contains:
authored terrain/elevation, a real recessed river and banks, a road/bridge system,
three role-differentiated Barrosan structures, one readable resource site, one
purpose-built hostile camp, and enough foliage/props to make the settlement feel
inhabited without clutter.
