# Ascendant Realms v0.377 Visual Reference Pack

## Purpose

This pack replaces text-only artistic guessing with four concrete visual targets.
It is not a collection of runtime assets. It is a binding art-direction reference
for original repository-authored Blender/Godot work.

The images define the intended relationships between:

- overall RTS composition;
- Barrosan architecture and role silhouettes;
- terrain, roads, riverbanks and bridge integration;
- gold-resource identity;
- hostile-camp identity;
- material hierarchy, environmental density and value structure.

## Files

### `01_FINAL_RTS_SECTOR_TARGET.png`

The primary aspiration image. Use it for:

- overall atmosphere and density;
- settlement-versus-hostile spatial story;
- terrain continuity;
- river depth and bank treatment;
- building role hierarchy;
- environmental clustering;
- warm Barrosan settlement versus harsher hostile territory.

Do not copy every decorative detail. The real implementation may be more
stylized and lower-poly. Preserve the composition principles and readable
silhouettes.

### `02_BARROSAN_BUILDING_LINEUP.png`

The binding silhouette and role reference for:

- Main Hall;
- House;
- Field Barracks.

The Main Hall must be tall and civic. The House must be low and domestic. The
Barracks must be long, broad and yard-oriented. They must remain distinguishable
in grayscale at normal RTS distance.

### `03_TERRAIN_ROAD_RIVER_BRIDGE.png`

The binding infrastructure reference. Use it for:

- uneven terrain shelves and rock-supported elevation;
- worn roads cut into the land;
- recessed river channel;
- irregular wet banks;
- stones, reeds and moss at water contact;
- stone-and-timber bridge abutments;
- natural road-to-bridge transitions.

The visible floating diorama edge in the concept sheet is only a presentation
convention. Approved gameplay-camera renders must conceal world boundaries.

### `04_GOLD_MINE_AND_HOSTILE_CAMP.png`

The binding functional-silhouette reference for:

- a gold mine that reads as an extraction site rather than yellow rocks;
- an Ashen Raider camp that reads as hostile territory rather than a market
  stall or civilian shelter.

## Interpretation hierarchy

When a detail conflicts across images, use this priority:

1. readability at normal RTS distance;
2. role silhouette;
3. terrain and object integration;
4. Barrosan or Ashen faction identity;
5. material hierarchy;
6. decorative detail.

## Detail level

The reference art is more richly detailed than the first production proof must
be. The implementation is not required to be photorealistic or match every
plank, stone or prop.

It is required to match the following visible qualities:

- no pale flat test board;
- no road or river ribbons;
- no showroom spacing;
- no generic same-shaped buildings;
- no resource made from loose bright rocks;
- no hostile camp made from civilian props;
- no giant low-information empty region;
- readable material and value separation;
- connected, inhabited environmental composition.

## Production order

The references must be implemented in this order:

1. terrain, road, riverbanks and bridge;
2. Main Hall, House and Barracks;
3. Gold Mine and Hostile Camp;
4. vegetation and functional dressing;
5. connected sector composition;
6. gameplay integration only after human visual approval.

The supplied v0.377 Codex prompt authorizes only Stage 1. Later stages require
separate checkpoints so weak assets cannot be hidden inside another full-map
render.
