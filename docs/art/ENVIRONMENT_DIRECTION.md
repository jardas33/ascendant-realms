# Environment direction

The battlefield should communicate land, road, water, bridge, settlement, resource site, and tactical lane through shape, value, material, and dressing. Roads should sit in the terrain; rivers should have believable banks and depth; buildings should own their ground; foreground decoration must not erase command readability.

The current visual debt and future asset pipeline are documented in [`../CINDERFEN_VISUAL_REWORK_SPEC.md`](../CINDERFEN_VISUAL_REWORK_SPEC.md) and [`../ASSET_PIPELINE_PLAN.md`](../ASSET_PIPELINE_PLAN.md). Those are direction and process documents, not proof that the gap is closed.
