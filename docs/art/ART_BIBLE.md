# Art bible

## Direction

Original dark heroic fantasy RTS/RPG: grounded highland terrain, strong silhouettes, practical materials, controlled atmosphere, and tactical readability. The hierarchy is battlefield readability first, atmosphere second, detail third.

## Rules

- silhouette and role before palette;
- terrain must support movement/readability rather than look like a board;
- buildings need mass, base, roof, side planes, and contextual ground;
- units need scale, feet/contact, ownership, and selection clarity;
- effects should explain action and consequence, not obscure the map;
- source, license, scale, and review status are recorded before runtime adoption.

The existing detailed Barrosan contract is [`V0236_BARROSAN_FACTION_ART_BIBLE.md`](V0236_BARROSAN_FACTION_ART_BIBLE.md). The current browser prototype is not yet the final art direction.
