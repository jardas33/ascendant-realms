## Documentary architecture decision

The selected path is `Path A — exterior-stair archetype`. The primary anchor is the complete source photograph `30987 Rustic porch in Montesinho (54961581200)`, chosen for its granite wall, exterior stair, landing, timber posts, upper opening, lower working bay, and slate eave relationship. Three substantial supplementary houses are retained on the `DOCUMENTARY ARCHITECTURE` board: `30985`, `30986`, and `França10`. The historical settlement image `30989` is kept only on the separate `ATMOSPHERE / SETTLEMENT MOOD` board and is not used as measured architecture evidence.

No documentary pixels, meshes, or protected game assets were imported into the Blender source.
