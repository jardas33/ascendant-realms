# Faction direction

Faction identity should come from economy, rhythm, unit roles, buildings, silhouettes, and strategic weakness—not recolor alone. Barrosan/Human direction is a practical frontier settlement with fieldstone, timber, weathered roofs, working yards, and readable military infrastructure. Lioraen direction must be authored separately and should not be inferred from placeholder color.

Current roster and campaign data remain data-driven under `src/game/data`. Any roster expansion needs stable IDs, content validation, visual provenance, and normal-play proof.
