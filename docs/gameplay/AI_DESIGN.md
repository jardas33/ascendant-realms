# AI direction

AI should create pressure that teaches the player what matters: economy, staging, attack timing, target choice, retreat, and defense. Difficulty should change planning and pressure, not hide causality.

Current AI lives in `src/game/ai` and is data/rules driven. The next AI work should begin with a bounded Easy Hollowspan rehearsal and a player-facing failure description. Do not add waves, factions, or behaviors while a basic command or result flow remains unreliable.
