# Player-experience pillars

1. **Orient:** know where I am, what the objective is, and what matters now.
2. **Choose:** understand available actions, costs, prerequisites, and risk.
3. **Act:** issue a command with conventional RTS input and visible intent feedback.
4. **Read:** see ownership, health, progress, threat, and terrain affordances.
5. **Adapt:** respond to pressure, recover from mistakes, and make a consequential choice.
6. **Conclude:** understand why the battle ended and what the next meaningful step is.

Every new feature should name the pillar it improves and the player-visible evidence that proves it.
