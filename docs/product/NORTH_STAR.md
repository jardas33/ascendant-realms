# Product north star

## Intent

Ascendant Realms is an original dark heroic-fantasy RTS/RPG hybrid. The player should feel that a personal hero leads a growing, faction-specific force through a reactive campaign world, where tactical decisions, preparation, combat, and consequences are understandable and worth repeating.

The long-term quality target is a desktop-quality strategy experience with the clarity of a good RTS and the persistence and identity of an RPG. It is a direction, not a claim that the current prototype already meets it.

## Product promises

1. **Readable decisions:** selection, orders, costs, production, threats, combat results, and consequences are visible without debug knowledge.
2. **Meaningful identity:** hero, faction, units, buildings, terrain, and campaign choices have distinct roles and visual language.
3. **A living route:** maps and campaign nodes create pressure, opportunity, and consequence without hiding the rules.
4. **Trustworthy persistence:** saves, stable IDs, rewards, and results remain deterministic and auditable.
5. **Original presentation:** broad genre conventions are allowed; copied protected assets or expression are not.

## Current reality

The active implementation is still a browser prototype. Its strongest evidence is systems and rules coverage, not a finished art or desktop-runtime experience. Future work must close player-facing gaps in priority order rather than treating historical version numbers as a quality guarantee.
