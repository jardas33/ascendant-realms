# Visual validation

Visual review asks whether the intended player can understand the scene at normal scale. Check 1920x1080 and 1366x768 for safe areas, text, cards, minimap, camera bounds, entity scale, ownership, health, selection, terrain lanes, and result states.

For a visual change, retain a before/after pair, a focused close view, a wide gameplay view, and a contact sheet only when the sheet remains readable. Automated pixel checks may catch regressions but do not replace human review.
