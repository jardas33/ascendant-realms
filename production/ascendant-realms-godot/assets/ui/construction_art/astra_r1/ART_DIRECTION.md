# Barrosan construction illustrations, Astra R1

These five transparent illustrations are used only by the compact worker build
cards. They do not replace the selected-building live 3D portrait or any world
model. The shared generation brief was: one complete, centered structure in an
elevated three-quarter view; polished painterly-realistic game UI illustration;
dark slate, weathered stone and timber, blue-and-gold Barrosan heraldry, and
warm directional light; legible at a 68 px card preview; transparent background;
no text, people, scenery, border, or watermark.
