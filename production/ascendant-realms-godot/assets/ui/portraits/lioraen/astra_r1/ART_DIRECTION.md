# Lioraen selected-unit portraits, R1

These portraits are UI-only artwork for the Seedkeeper and Grove Warden. They
replace the isolated 3D T-pose in selected-unit and matching training-card
portraits; live character models, animation, gameplay data, and saves remain
unchanged.

Shared generation brief: square, chest-up three-quarter fantasy RTS portrait;
face and eyes readable in a 150 px HUD frame; premium grounded painterly
realism; tactile skin and cloth; pale living wood, forest green, jade/teal,
small white blossoms, and restrained amber details; quiet blue-green woodland
background. No full-body pose, UI border, text, logo, or watermark.
